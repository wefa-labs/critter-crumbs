## 3. Music and expressive feedback

The existing player deliberately overlaps successive passes by two seconds: inspect `audio/music-plan.ts` and `audio/playback.ts`. Equal-power fades smooth level; they do not guarantee musical continuity. The user hears the repeat boundary.

Inspect all four loop assets in `packages/app/public/audio/music/`: `burrow.mp3`, `square.mp3`, `feeding.mp3`, and `full.mp3`. The feast and its resolve are separate cues, not ordinary loops. `audio/assets.ts` maps them, and `docs/research/audio-assets.md` records sourcing/processing. Locate original masters/stems if present; state when only encoded files are available.

Retain the settled direction: warm woodwind chamber ensemble, marimba/vibraphone, pizzicato, upright bass and brushes, light swing, and singable melodies. The requested Nintendo/theme-park quality is a feel reference; use original music. Do not recreate recognisable melodies or introduce paid generation without explicit authorization.

For each track:

- Audition several repetitions of the existing version. Describe the specific seam: silence, doubled beat, restarted phrase, overlapping harmony, or a volume change. Do not claim listening proof from waveform analysis alone.
- Propose a bar/phrase-aligned loop with compatible start/end harmony and a controlled reverb tail. Preserve level/headroom and avoid a click at the exact boundary.
- Supply a local audition with at least three repeats, plus proposed loop start/end in seconds and sample-frame indices with the sample rate. Explain any short tail treatment; do not prescribe a blanket two-second overlap.
- Distinguish seamless repetition from intentional crossfades between different screens. Codex will implement the approved per-track playback metadata, scheduling, cleanup, visibility behavior, and tests.

Also review the leaf/crumb notification treatment, receiving/reversing crumbs, and results huddle for coherent responses. Feedback should clarify what happened, feel welcoming, and remain understandable muted. Avoid shaming low totals or missed scattering. Every sound pairs with a visible DOM state, and expressive motion has a reduced-motion equivalent.
