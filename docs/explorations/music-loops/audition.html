Music loop auditions

Each track three ways: as the app loops it today, as proposed, and an alternative. Every file plays four passes, so it holds three seams; the seam buttons start six seconds before one. Files are at their shipped level (−16 LUFS), not the app's music level. The numbers are measurements, not listening: see the record. Nothing plays until you press play.
