Backgrounds to paint

Today the circle stands on a floor cropped out of the paintings and tilted onto a plane, which blurs it. One painted plate per scene and device replaces that: the floor fills the frame and the wall runs only along the top, as in a top-down game room. The prompts keep the scene style the three paintings were made in (design directions §15).

Tools, in the order to try them

  • Midjourney V8.2 for the painting itself. It is still the strongest at painterly looks, and the earlier scenes came from it. Give the existing paintings as style references (--sref) so the plates match them; --raw makes it follow the layout more literally, --hd renders at 2048 px. V8 replaced --oref with the Edit model (up to four reference images) and --style raw with --raw.
  • Nano Banana Pro (Gemini, or AI Studio) to turn an existing painting into the top-down view, or to fix a Midjourney plate by instruction ("move the table into the top-left corner"). It takes up to three style references and renders 9:16, 16:9 and 21:9 up to 4K.
  • GPT Images 2.5 (ChatGPT) when the layout must be exact: it follows placement instructions best, renders any size (1024 × 2048 for the phone), and edits by comment without repainting the rest.
  • Scenario once four to eight plates are approved: train a style model on them (the paintings are WEFA's own) to paint the seasons and later scenes in exactly the same hand. Paid plans include a commercial licence.
  • Photoshop Generative Expand (Firefly Image 5) to widen or heighten an approved plate for another screen size rather than repainting it.

Paint each plate at twice the frame (750 × 1500 for the phone, 2560 × 1440 for desktop) and export WebP. Use a paid plan for anything that ships, and note the tool beside each file.

Pass eleven: your crumbs in the middle, desktop that scales
Built from the review of pass ten (5 October 2026), on Inked daylight. Same seed, strings and looks in every frame.

    
    
      
  • Your crumb pile in the middle. On both devices the crumbs you have left heap in the middle of the circle, about twice the size they were, drawn by the same rule as the critters' piles (the Piles control switches both). The heap keeps its place as you give, shrinking toward its base, and takes the height in the middle that covers least of the critters and tags around it. Empty, it leaves three faint outlines. The count stays in the bar, with Scatter These, Scattered. and Results after it, as before.
    • 
      
  • Crumbs fly both ways. A crumb hops from the heap to the critter's pile when you give, and from the pile back onto the heap when you take one back; a hold pours a stream either way. On the phone, while the statement covers the heap, the crumbs fly from the crumb in the bar.
    • 
      
  • Your own critter stirs. A tap on it no longer turns the circle to it: it turns over in its sleep with a little squash and three z's drift up, and the critter in focus stays in focus. At the feast a tap still brings any critter into focus.
    • 
      
  • Desktop controls centred. − count + sits centred under the critter in focus again (pass ten's dock change had pushed it to the left).
    • 
      
  • The focused critter's tag on desktop. It shows the whole name, a size larger, without the count, which sits between − and + right under it.
    • 
      
  • Whole names. A tag shows the whole name (up to 16 characters) wherever it fits, and the 10-character form only where the whole name would cover a neighbour or a tag still to be placed. On desktop at 1440 and 1920 every name fits whole.
    • 
      
  • No background behind the statement. On desktop the statement sits straight on the side panel.
    • 
      
  • Results buttons in one row on the phone. Replay Feast and Download sit side by side at the end of the results, half the width each. Download still reads as "Download CSV" to a screen reader. Desktop says Replay Feast too, and keeps Download CSV.
    • 
      
  • A bar for the results on desktop. With Results open, Replay Feast and Download CSV sit in a bar at the foot of the side panel, level with the folded dock, while the list scrolls above it under a fade.
    • 
      
  • Bigger critters that scale. On desktop everything in the circle scales with the floor: at 1280 × 800 the critter in focus is 248 px (was 190) and the rest about a quarter larger; at 1440 × 900 and 1920 × 1080 they grow with the screen. Type and controls keep their size. The side panel is 400 px, and a quarter of the width on screens wider than 1600 px, up to 480. The Size control switches the desktop frame between the three sizes. With Results open the critters huddle larger on the folded dock.
    • 
      
  • From pass ten. Results grows out of the dock on the phone, the critters huddle on its edge and play at random; tags centred above; a focus row that never changes height; piles beside each critter (Proportional or Exact); "You" on your critter; names up to 16 characters.
    • 
      
  • Still to design. A short first-run hint that a tap on a critter gives a crumb and a hold pours (the existing scatter.instruction string); it goes into the onboarding on the screens page.
    • 
      
  • Still open. Taking Inked daylight to the app; the gold button against D3; Proportional or Exact piles; the floor plate; decision lines for the circle in place of the grid (§6.2, §6.3), tokens (§3) and renderer version 2 (D8).
