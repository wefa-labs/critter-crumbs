# Copy, privacy sweep and documents

One of the areas of the [October QA pass](../README.md). 17 findings; of 27 things checked, 16 passed, 11 failed.

I checked every word a member can read or have read out, what leaves the browser, what stays in it, and the documents the rehearsal will be run from. The wording is clean: none of the six banned words in 357 strings, in the source, in 21 fixture states at two sizes in both engines, or in about 90 live screens, and every string comes from the copy file. Nothing phones home: 4,407 requests on the dev build and 123 from the built bundle all went to the app's own origin, the bundle's one outside call is the documented key fetch from Shutter when the store fails, the server logs nothing, and the browser keeps four settings, a session cookie and a receipt per scatter. Two things should be mended before the feast: when no scatter can be read the results say "Nobody scattered" and hide that scatters were saved (copy-01), and the creator's Remove from burrow has no route file on Vercel (copy-02, not yet tried on a deployment). The rest is small and cheap: "1 critters", a number missing from one refusal, a name cut on narrow phones, deadline lines in mixed languages, a wrong line in a Shutter outage, and a run sheet whose step 8 cannot be done and whose step 3 leads to a dead end (copy-10). The specification tables are behind the build in places, none of which changes what the app does.

## Findings
