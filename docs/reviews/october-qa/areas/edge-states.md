# Edge and error states

One of the areas of the [October QA pass](../README.md). 20 findings; of 28 things checked, 20 passed, 8 failed.

I took all 28 edge states of Technical Spec §15 through the app on the edge-states dev stack at 390x844, in Chromium and again in WebKit, making outages, lost replies, expired sessions, wrong clocks, missing WebGL and altered data with request interception, the mock clock and small stand-ins where the mock stack cannot produce a state by itself. In every state the feast still completed, nothing that had been saved was lost, and in every honest run all members held the same result; 20 states behave as their rows say and 8 fail in some part. Three things should be fixed before the council uses it: when the game drops out of 3D its notice pushes the scatter screen sideways off the phone, as a production build draws it; on the gathering and burrow pages a failed save's message lands below the bottom of the screen; and a member still writing when gathering closes loses the words with no message, or with one saying scattering has closed. One needs a decision: a device that receives altered scatters opens the feast with a different result and no warning. The rest is wording and placement: the clock and ENS notices are never drawn, "The key isn't out yet" turns into an outage message after 20 seconds, a late joiner is invited to scatter before being turned away, and three setup errors do not say what is wrong. None of this ran on the production bundle, and Shutter, a wallet, Base and ENS were all stand-ins.

- **Where:** Waiting screen. packages/app/src/app/results.tsx:44 sets feast.keyNotYet without clearing the 20-second timer of line 37, which then sets err.keyUnreachable and raises the app alert.
- **Expected:** docs/04-technical-spec.md §15 E28: "feast.keyNotYet; nothing decrypts" and "Wait". E1 keeps "err.keyUnreachable after 20 s" for "Shutter unreachable at reveal".
- **Observed:** Chromium: "0.9s key notice "The key isn't out yet. Try again in a few minutes." [Try again] → 20.6s waiting notice "Can't fetch the key right now.
