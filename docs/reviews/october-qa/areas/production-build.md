# Production build

One of the areas of the [October QA pass](../README.md). 16 findings; of 15 things checked, 12 passed, 2 failed, 1 could not be reached.

I ran the production bundle in dist/ on the production-build stack, under the headers vercel.json sets and nothing else, in Chromium and WebKit, connecting through injected wallets that sign in Node. Both of yesterday's fixes hold: the app starts on all 20 kinds of address with no policy violation and no page error in either engine, every chunk loads cold and in any order (96 cases), Connect reaches the wallet, and the Koota patch behaves as the generated code did for all 25 of the app's traits. Through the bundle a new member can sign in, take the first run, create a burrow, add and join members, set up and open a feast, write statements, see the reveal, read, and fill the scatter screen in 3D and in Simple view; the save is then refused with "Couldn't save your scatter. Check your connection and try again" without the wallet being asked, and a finished feast stays on "The feast is being laid out" (lifecycle-01, already known). Fixing that is more than removing a check: with the real Shutter client called from src/crypto the build's own checks refuse the bundle twice (the chunk ring that broke Connect comes back), no scatter encrypted for a real identity has ever been opened with a released key, and recording a public result exists only for the rehearsal accounts; the client crypto itself does run under this policy in both engines, which I showed for the first time. New and not on the known list: a page loaded before a new deployment goes blank when it opens a feast, and a person with no wallet in their browser is told only "No connection is available in this browser yet." No request left the stack in any run, nothing meant for development ships except unused copy, and on this bundle WebKit held 60 frames a second where the dev stacks' version 2 critters gave 4 to 7.
