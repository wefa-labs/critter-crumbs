# Lifecycle

One of the areas of the [October QA pass](../README.md). 20 findings; of 29 things checked, 22 passed, 5 failed, 1 could not be reached, 1 were not run.

I ran the repository's e2e and screens suites in Chromium and in WebKit, then took three burrows through the screens with four members in separate windows at once (WebKit twice, Chromium once; a people feast and a four-option feast each; phones at 390x844 with touch, desktops at 1440x900), checking every fingerprint, total and CSV. On the dev stacks a feast runs from start to finish for every member in both engines: in all six feasts the four members computed the same result, the CSV matched it, no statement or scatter leaked or was lost, and there were no page errors. Four things should be fixed before the council uses it: the production bundle is built to refuse scattering and opening a feast; on desktop the dock with Results collapses once the feast has played; on phone browsers under about 800 px tall the name tags leave their critters and cover each other; and an options feast names members by address. While scattering is open nothing says who has scattered, which PRD R5 asks for and is Afolabi's call. Apart from speed WebKit behaved as Chromium did, but it draws the dev stacks' version 2 critters at 4 to 7 frames a second and its screenshots cannot show the floor's perspective, so both need a real iPhone.

## Not run

- A whole feast on the production bundle: no production stack is in my brief, and the bundle cannot scatter or open a feast (lifecycle-01).
- The game as production draws it (generator version 1 with 3D meshes): the dev stacks draw version 2 portraits only, so 3D on a feast screen and Simple view's effect were never seen (lifecycle-16).
- A real finger held on + in WebKit: Playwright's WebKit can tap but not hold a touch. Holds were made with the pointer there, and with real touch events in Chromium.
- A real wallet: every connection and signature used the rehearsal critters. The switch to Base Sepolia, a declined signature in a wallet, and the second signature after a reload were not exercised.
- The floor's perspective in WebKit: Playwright's WebKit screenshots flatten CSS 3D, shown on a bare page (probe-perspective.mjs).
- "The key isn't out yet" and every outage or error state: the dev build opens the feast with the mock key by itself, and those states belong to the edge-states area.
- A Chromium journey with the people feast's creator on a phone (variant A), left out for time and to spare the machine.
- A burrow of six to ten through the screens: each journey had five critters (four connected, one who never connects). Eight critters were only seen in seeded feasts and fixtures.
- Reading the clipboard in WebKit, so "Copy burrow link" and "Copy prompt" are confirmed there only by their announcements.
- tests/screens/reference.spec.ts (needs the review-page server on port 4184).
- Anything audible: every browser came from .scratch/qa/browser.mjs or a silenced stack, by the harness's rule.
