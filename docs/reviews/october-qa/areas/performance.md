# Performance

One of the areas of the [October QA pass](../README.md). 9 findings; of 9 things checked, 7 passed, 2 failed.

All nine items have a status, from three agents' measurements on this tree (the morning's and the afternoon's of 10 October, and mine of 11 October, named in each note). On the production bundle the budgets that can be counted hold: the launcher is 217 kB in 8 requests with no sound and no audio context before a gesture, the crypto chunk and Shutter's curve library load only when needed, a ten-critter scene is 36 draw calls and 17,202 triangles, a held pour ran at 42 to 45 frames a second under 4x CPU slowdown, 100 crumbs pour in 3.4 s, the feast plays in 7.1 to 7.2 s with Skip from the first frame, the reveal yields between scatters, and the ladder ends in Simple view announced once. No blocker and no major. Four things do not match the spec: the read pass loads and draws 3D; with ten critters the tenth is never drawn in 3D, and it is the same member's critter on everyone's screen; with 3D on the render loop never goes on demand, so Still critters, reduced motion and the ladder's first step all keep drawing 60 frames a second; and a public feast after close is not cacheable. The first tap starts 1.84 MB of sound, which on a slow connection holds up what the tap asked for (Connect to the wallet list: 4.5 s against 1.6 s on Slow 4G). Every duration and frame rate was taken on a desktop at load 5 to 34 with emulated throttling and needs a phone or a quiet machine; the counts (bytes, requests, draw calls, frames drawn, DOM nodes) do not.

## Findings

### performance-03

**In a feast of ten, the tenth critter is never drawn in 3D: it stays a flat portrait, and it is the same critter on every member's screen.**

- **Severity:** minor. **Call:** fix. **The finder's confidence:** confirmed. **Engines:** Chromium (Metal), WebKit; 390x844 and 1280x800.
- **State:** Open. A council of ten would see it: one member's critter stays flat on every screen.
- **Where:** Scatter screen and results with ten critters, production bundle. packages/app/src/app/renderer.tsx:23 takes visible.slice(0, Math.floor((RENDER_BUDGET.sceneCalls-1)/RENDER_BUDGET.callsPerCritter)), which is floor(39 / 4) = 9 (packages/shared/src/feel.ts:56: callsPerCritter 4, sceneCalls 40).
- **Expected:** docs/03-game-design.md section 6, Draw calls: "≤ 4 per critter after merging parts per material (base, accent, eyes, blob); ≤ 40 in the scene, plus one for the crumb instances"; Triangles: "≤ 3,000 per critter; ≤ 30,000 in the scene", with the note "10 critters". Ten critters at four calls each are the 40; the crumbs' one call is on top of it. docs/02-design-system.md D6: "portraits render first and crossfade to 3D".
- **Observed:** Mine, 02c-tenth-p5.log, three members of one ten-member feast: "viewer Fern (key 2): 9 meshes on 10 stages · in order: Clover, Fern, Jules, Pebble, Moss, Acorn, Hazel, Willow, Juniper, Indigo (FLAT) · ever flat while the circle went all the way round: Indigo", and the same line for viewers Hazel and Indigo. Morning, 02b-budgets-chromium.log and 02b-budgets-webkit.log: "10 stages, 9 meshes; left as portraits: stage 10 of 10 (Indigo)" and "after turning the circle ten times, the stages ever left as portraits: 10 (Indigo)". Results are the same (afternoon and mine, 07-reveal logs: "meshReady":9,"stages":10). The code counts the crumbs' draw call inside the 40 and so stops at nine. In a council of ten, one member's critter is the flat one for everybody for the whole feast; by the code (packages/app/src/sim/stage-runtime.ts, hop) crumbs given to it take the DOM path and not the 3D one, which was not watched. With nine or fewer every critter is 3D. The scene has room: 36 calls and 17,202 triangles for nine.
- **To reproduce:** From the repository root: mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/make-feast.mjs prod p5 10 people scattering 15000 28800000 ; mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/02c-tenth.mjs p5 2,5,9

### performance-04

**With 3D on, the render loop never goes on demand: Still critters, reduced motion and the ladder's first step all keep drawing every frame.**

- **Severity:** minor. **Call:** accept. **The finder's confidence:** confirmed. **Engines:** Chromium (Metal) and WebKit, 390x844 at pixel density 3.
- **Where:** Scatter screen and results in 3D, production bundle. Likely cause, from reading and not proved by a test: packages/app/src/three/runtime.ts:60 gives the canvas frameloop: 'always' as a fixed prop (packages/app/src/three/scene.tsx:15), and @react-three/fiber 9.7.0 puts the prop back on every render of the canvas ("if (state.frameloop !== frameloop) state.setFrameloop(frameloop)"), undoing the 'demand' or 'never' that runtime.ts:39 set. In Simple view, where there is no canvas, the same ticker does stop.
- **Expected:** docs/03-game-design.md section 6, Render loop: "Continuous while critters are visible and awake; on demand (invalidate) when every visible critter is asleep or Still critters is on; paused when the tab is hidden or the grid is scrolled fully out of view"; the ladder, step 1: "pixel ratio to 1.0 and idle motion off". docs/04-technical-spec.md section 17: "R3F `frameloop` switches per Game Design §6".
- **Observed:** Mine, 06b-loop-p5.log, exact counts over three 4-second windows a state (load 11 to 18). Defaults: "WebGL frames drawn 54.1 / 59.9 / 59.7 /s of 54.1 / 59.9 / 59.9 display frames (36 / 36 / 36.2 calls a frame)". Still critters turned on in Settings: "59.9 / 41.6 / 56.7 /s of 59.9 / 41.6 / 56.7 display frames", with DOM changes up from about 1,680 to about 3,060 a second. A new page with Still critters already on: "59.9 / 59.7 / 59.9 /s of 59.9 / 59.9 / 59.9". A new page with prefers-reduced-motion: "60.2 / 59.9 / 60.1 /s". Main-thread time is about the same as with motion (188 to 299 against 223 to 325 ms a second on this Mac). For comparison, Simple view with Still critters: "frames asked 0 / 0 / 0 /s · DOM changes 3 / 3 / 4.5 /s". Mine, 06c-ladder-idle-cpu12.log: after step 1 of the ladder with nothing touched, "WebGL drew on 21.6 of 21.6 display frames a second (36 calls a frame)", then 16.9 of 16.9 and 22.7 of 22.7 at 12x, and 60 of 60 back at 1x. A hidden-tab event stops the simulation (DOM changes fall to 542 a second) but not the drawing ("59.9 / 60.1 / 59.9 /s"); a really hidden tab gets no animation frames from the browser, so that part costs nothing. WebKit, mine, 06b-loop-p5-webkit.log, two windows a state (load 15 to 22): defaults "WebGL frames drawn 60 / 60.1 /s of 60 / 60.1 display frames (36 / 36 calls a frame)", Still critters on "58.7 / 60.1 /s of 58.7 / 60.1 display frames", the hidden-tab event "59.9 / 59.9 /s". Afternoon, 06-idle-metal-cpu1.log, one run: the same 2,160 draw calls a second in every 3D state. Nothing a member sees is wrong: with Still critters on nothing moves. The cost is battery and heat for exactly the members who asked for less, and a first ladder step that lowers the pixel ratio but not the frame count. Accept for October because the cure turns on a mode these measurements never saw running with 3D, which wants its own testing.
- **To reproduce:** From the repository root: mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/06b-loop.mjs p5 3 4 ; mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/06c-ladder-idle.mjs 12 p5 ; mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/06b-loop.mjs p5 2 4 webkit

### performance-01

- **Severity:** minor. **Call:** decide. **The finder's confidence:** confirmed. **Engines:** Chromium.
- **Where:** /feasts/:id/read on the production bundle. The read pass is a state of the scatter screen, and packages/app/src/app/scatter.tsx:144 mounts the 3D renderer for every state of it.
- **Expected:** docs/03-game-design.md section 6, 3D chunk: "never loaded on the arrival or read screens". docs/01-prd.md section 9: "The landing, arrival, and statement screens must not pay for the 3D bundle." docs/02-design-system.md, D6 (line 325): "This keeps the read pass free of WebGL entirely".

### performance-05

**Each time the 3D scene is mounted, its canvas and WebGL context object are never released.**

- **Severity:** note. **Call:** accept. **The finder's confidence:** confirmed. **Engines:** Chromium (Metal), WebKit.
- **Where:** Leaving the scatter screen or results, and each rebuild of the scene inside them, production bundle. The scene's own dispose is in packages/app/src/three/runtime.ts (useStageDriver); what still holds the canvas afterwards was not traced.
- **Expected:** The brief, item 5: track anything that only grows. No line of the spec covers it. docs/03-game-design.md section 6 gives "GPU memory ≤ 60 MB", which this does not touch: the context is lost on leaving.
- **Observed:** Mine, 05d-canvas-held-chromium.log, twelve visits home, scatter, home with a forced garbage collection before each reading: "from "home after visit 2" to "home after visit 12" (10 visits): nodes 738 → 768 (3.0 a visit) · listeners 496 → 496 (0.0 a visit) · heap 9.1 → 10.6 MB (154 kB a visit) · canvases held 2 → 12"; every context made is still held and none is live after leaving. The three nodes are the canvas and its two wrappers. Morning, 05-memory-20min-metal-3d.log: 58 canvases made in 20 rounds (three a round), one live, nodes 1,153 to 1,326, which is the nine a round the preliminary report could not explain; heap still rising about 0.13 MB a round at the end. Too small to matter in a feast: a few megabytes after dozens of visits. The afternoon's 05c-canvas-leak.mjs shows 555 nodes a visit; that is its own DevTools listing holding the nodes it lists, which 05d does not use.

### performance-06

**A public feast after close is sent no-store; the spec gives it a 60-second public cache.**

- **Severity:** note. **Call:** accept. **The finder's confidence:** confirmed. **Engines:** none (API only).
- **Where:** GET /api/feasts/:id for a feast with aggregatePublic, after close. packages/api/src/http.ts:6 sets 'Cache-Control': 'no-store' on every JSON reply, and vercel.json sets no-store on /api/(.*).
- **Expected:** docs/04-technical-spec.md section 17, Caching: "API responses `Cache-Control: no-store` except `GET /feasts/:id` for public feasts after close (`public, max-age=60`)."

Either the sentence in the spec goes or the header is added.
- **To reproduce:** From the repository root: mise exec node@22.22.1 -- node .scratch/qa/performance/08d-public-feast.mjs (it makes and closes its own public feast and moves the production stack's clock about 36 s on)
